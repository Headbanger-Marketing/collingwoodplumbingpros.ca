Questions About the Work

What was removed or poured into the drain before this request?

A slow drain after owner work may involve a changed section, a joint or product residue, so the order of events helps a provider decide where to look.

  • Start with the branch the owner touched

    The respondent's first job is to establish which branch or section was touched, whether it serves one fixture or several, and where the main drain lies relative to it. Give them the trap or pipe material if you know it, the date of the work, any product used and photos of the area. If a fixture backs up, stop using it.

  • Compare cleaning the line with checking the change

    Compare clearing the blockage alone with having the respondent look at the changed section as well, for example by camera. Ask what a look at the changed section would add to clearing the line, and what the provider would report about anything found there.

  • Keep a record of what was removed

    Once work is agreed, request a note of what was removed from the line, which section was cleared and what the provider saw at the changed joint. Photograph anything handed back to you at your Collingwood home.
